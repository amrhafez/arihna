Questions

Times differ from the mosque by a minute?

In Egypt, Arihna follows the Egyptian General Authority for Survey's timetable for your city, and its method on days the timetable does not yet cover. A mosque may round or adjust by a minute.

Which direction is the Qibla from Hurghada?

134.9° from true north (southeast), along the shortest path to the Kaaba over the round Earth.
