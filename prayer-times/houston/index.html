Questions

Times differ from the mosque by a minute?

There is no single official timetable in the US. Arihna calculates with the ISNA method; your mosque may use another method or its own timetable. In the app, Match my mosque fits Arihna to your mosque's times.

Which direction is the Qibla from Houston?
