Questions

Times differ from the mosque by a minute?

There is no single official timetable in the UK. Arihna calculates with the Moonsighting Committee method; your mosque may use another method or its own timetable. In the app, Match my mosque fits Arihna to your mosque's times.

Which direction is the Qibla from Birmingham?

117.8° from true north (east-southeast), along the shortest path to the Kaaba over the round Earth.
