Privacy policy

Arihna (أرحنا) app and website · Effective 28 October 2026

Data Not Collected
No accountNo adsNo tracking

The short version

  • Nothing leaves your phone. Times, Qibla and moon are all calculated on your iPhone; nothing you do in the app is sent to us or anyone else.

Location

  • Optional: only used if you choose “My location”.

  • Stays on your phone: kept inside the app and its widgets, never sent to us or anyone else.

  • Area name: to show your area (for example Al Bahia), iOS asks Apple's own location service once, as Maps does.

  • Your choice: pick one of the eight UAE cities and never share it at all.

Compass and motion

  • Read live only while the Qibla screen is open, to turn the dial.

  • Never stored and never sent anywhere.

Notifications and Live Activity

  • Scheduled on your phone by iOS itself.

  • No push server is involved.

Data we collect

  • None. The App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected”.

  • Website: hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep standard server logs under its own privacy statement.

Children

  • All ages: the app collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

  • Published here with a new date whenever this policy changes.
